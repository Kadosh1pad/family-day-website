A Celebration of Love, Faith & Unity

Family Day is a special day where church brings people of all ages together 🏛️.

Parents, kids, teenagers, youths and servers sit and pray side by side instead of going to different rooms 🙏.

After the service, everyone shares good food and plays fun games outside 🎲🍽️.

Most of all, it reminds us that church is one big family ready to care for each other ❤️.
